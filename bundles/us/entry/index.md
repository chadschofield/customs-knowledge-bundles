# Entry

How goods are entered into the United States: pathways, the de minimis
suspension that reshaped them in 2025–2026, the postal processes replacing it,
and the machinery (entry summary, right to make entry, bonds) underneath.

* [Entry Types & Pathways](entry-types.md) - Formal vs informal entry and the entry-type codes that matter for low-value shipments.
* [De Minimis Suspension](de-minimis-suspension.md) - Status, full timeline, surviving exemptions, the proposed permanent regime (NPRM), and the statutory repeal on 2027-07-01.
* [Postal Informal Entry (IMDW)](postal-informal-entry-imdw.md) - Mandatory mail entry from 2026-07-24: 14-field worksheet, broker filing, bonds, monthly deadlines; PGA/Ch. 98-99 cliff now 2027-01-20.
* [Entry Type 13 Test](entry-type-13-test.md) - Voluntary electronic informal mail entry, live in ACE since 2026-09-22; CBP's intended replacement for the worksheet.
* [Postal Returns, Refunds & Drawback](postal-returns-refunds-drawback.md) - Refused or undeliverable mail: duty refund when returned from custody (§§ 145.5, 145.71, 158.45) vs Part 190 drawback after release; the open gap for IMDW/ET13 entries.
* [Entry Summary in ACE](entry-summary-ace.md) - CBP Form 7501 data in ACE: filing, statements, post-summary corrections, liquidation.
* [Right to Make Entry](right-to-make-entry.md) - Owner, purchaser, or licensed customs broker — and how consignees must appoint brokers.
* [Customs Bonds](bonds.md) - Bond types and where each is now mandatory for low-value entry.
