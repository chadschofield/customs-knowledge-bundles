The administrative *de minimis* exemption (19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(C)) allowed
duty-free entry of shipments valued at **$800 or less** with minimal data. It
is now **suspended for all modes of transport and all countries**, and will be
**permanently repealed by statute on 2027-07-01** (One Big Beautiful Bill Act,
Pub. L. 119-21, § 70531(b)). Rules in force today are interim final rules with
open comment periods — verify dates before relying on them.

# Status Snapshot (as of 2026-07-03)

| Item | Status | Next change |
|------|--------|-------------|
| De minimis ($800, all modes) | **Suspended** | Statutory repeal 2027-07-01 |
| Non-postal low-value pathway | **Entry Type 11** or formal; ET86 and release-from-manifest eliminated (2026-06-24) | — |
| Postal pathway | EO 14324 interim QP spreadsheet | **IMDW** worksheet from 2026-07-24; [ET13 test](/entry/entry-type-13-test.md) from 2026-09-22 |
| Misuse penalties (OBBBA) | $5,000 first / $10,000 subsequent violation (since 2025-08-03) | — |

# What Survives

| Exemption | Limit | Authority |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Bona fide gifts (person-to-person) | ≤ $100 ($200 from certain US island possessions) | 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(A); 19 CFR 10.152 |
| Travelers' accompanying personal/household articles | ≤ $200 | 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(B) |
| IEEPA statutory carve-outs (personal communications; food/medicine donations; informational materials) | n/a | 50 U.S.C. 1702(b) |

Caveats: a gift arriving **non-postal** must still be claimed on an Entry
Type 11 filing (express carriers may decline to offer this due to cost), and
**business-to-consumer ecommerce shipments never qualify** as bona fide gifts.

# Operational Consequences

- Every low-value shipment now needs a **10-digit HTSUS classification**, a
  **[bond](/entry/bonds.md)**, and full duty payment.
- Mail requires a **licensed US customs broker** as filer from 2026-07-24.
- Declared-value accuracy is an enforcement focus —
  [EO 14411](/enforcement/eo-14411-enforcement.md) sets a 50% penalty floor
  and eliminates mitigation for repeat offenders.

# Known Data Discrepancies

- The EO 14324 effective date is 2025-08-29 (12:01 a.m. EDT) in the EO text;
  some CBP implementation material references 2025-08-30. Use 2025-08-29.
- One internal report's diagram states the Section 122 surcharge as 15%; the
  primary source (FR 2026-12669) says **10%**. Use 10%.
