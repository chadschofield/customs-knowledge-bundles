A customs bond is a surety contract guaranteeing CBP the payment of duties,
taxes, and fees and compliance with entry obligations (authority: 19 U.S.C.
1623; regulations: 19 CFR Part 113). Since the
[de minimis suspension](/entry/de-minimis-suspension.md), **no low-value
pathway works without one** — bonds moved from a formal-entry concern to a
universal parcel-logistics requirement.

# Forms of Bond

| Form | Scope | Typical use |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **Single Transaction Bond (STB)** | One entry | Occasional importers, high-risk shipments |
| **Continuous Bond** | All entries by the principal for a year, self-renewing | Regular importers; minimum coverage historically $50,000 (10% of annual duties rule of thumb) |

Bonds are written against **activity codes** — the ones that matter here:

| Activity | Regulation | Who needs it |
|----------|------------|--------------|
| Basic importation and entry (code 1) | 19 CFR 113.62 | Importers of record for formal entry, Entry Type 11, the [postal IMDW process](/entry/postal-informal-entry-imdw.md) (new 19 CFR 145.15), and the [ET13 test](/entry/entry-type-13-test.md). No release from CBP custody without it. |
| International carrier (code 3) | 19 CFR 113.64 | Any carrier transporting international postal shipments to the US, per EO 14324 — secures duty collection on mail. |
| Custodian of bonded merchandise (code 2) | 19 CFR 113.63 | Bonded carriers, warehouses, container stations. |

Cash in lieu of surety is possible (19 CFR 113.40). Bond data is transmitted
via **eBond** to ACE — record formats in
[eBond Filing (CB/CX)](/ace-filing/ebond-filing.md) — or filed with CBP's
Office of Finance — Revenue Division (details: ESBP Chapter 3).

# Who Is Obligated in the Broker-as-IOR Model

When a consignee (foreign postal operator, USPS, forwarder, carrier) appoints
a licensed broker who acts as importer of record — the standard
[ET13 pattern](/entry/entry-type-13-test.md) — **the broker's own bond is
obligated**. Brokers should price and risk-manage accordingly.

# Coming Changes — EO 14411

[EO 14411](/enforcement/eo-14411-enforcement.md) directs DHS (deadline
2026-11-30) to set **minimum asset and bonding levels for importers of
record**, restrict foreign IORs' use of continuous bonds (formal entry only
with CBP authorization), and raise bond requirements on high-risk shipments.
Expect rulemaking that changes bond sufficiency math for foreign shippers.
