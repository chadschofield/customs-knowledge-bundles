CBP is testing **Entry Type 13 — Informal Mail Entry**, an electronic informal
entry filed in ACE for international mail valued at **$2,500 or less**
(general notice FR 2026-12668, published 2026-06-24; test begins
**2026-09-22** under 19 CFR 101.9(a) test authority). It is the automated
alternative to the emailed [IMDW worksheet](/entry/postal-informal-entry-imdw.md)
and is CBP's stated intended permanent replacement for it. The test runs until
CBP concludes it by Federal Register notice.

# Why It Matters

- **Automation**: replaces monthly email spreadsheets with ACE/ABI electronic
  filing (technical specs in the [CATAIR](https://www.cbp.gov/trade/ace/catair)).
- **Unlocks restricted goods**: waives the formal-entry requirement for mail
  subject to **PGA data requirements** (19 CFR 145.12(a)(2)(v)) and **HTSUS
  Chapter 98/99 duties** (19 CFR 145.12(a)(2)(vi)) — the goods that fall off
  the IMDW at the 2026-10-22 compliance cliff.
- **Still excluded**: AD/CVD and quota merchandise must use formal entry
  (19 CFR 145.12(a)). No waiver.

# Who May Participate

| Party | Role |
|-------|------|
| Owner or purchaser of the mailed goods | May file directly (right to make entry) |
| Licensed US customs broker | Files when appointed by owner, purchaser, or consignee |
| Consignees (foreign postal operators, USPS, freight forwarders, carriers) | Must hire a licensed broker to act as **importer of record**; the broker's bond is obligated |
| Carriers transporting mail | May voluntarily report the foreign-post tracking number on the manifest; CBP matches it to the entry to verify arrival time and timely filing |

Bond: basic importation & entry bond (STB or continuous) under 19 CFR 113.62
— see [Customs Bonds](/entry/bonds.md).

# The 12 Data Elements (transmitted in ACE by the IOR)

1. Filer Code (validates licensed-broker status)
2. IOR Number
3. Description of Merchandise
4. Country of Origin
5. All applicable 10-digit HTSUS classification(s) — primary (Ch. 1–97) and
   secondary (Ch. 98/99) where applicable
6. Quantity and Weight — **only** if a specific duty rate applies
7. Duty Rate
8. Value
9. Total Duty Owed
10. Carrier Name
11. Tracking Number (generated by the foreign post operator)
12. Arrival Port

# Implementation in ACE (as of 2026-08-03)

CBP is building ET13 as two ACE enhancements — **INT-057** (U.S. Mail
Processing) and **CBP-290** (Mail Entry Type 13 Test) — deployed to the
**Certification environment 2026-07-24** and to **Production 2026-09-22**,
confirming the test start date (CSMS # 69289734; the
[Trade Information Notice](https://www.cbp.gov/document/guidance/trade-information-notice-implementation-entry-type-13-test-ace-us-mail-processing)
has the deployment detail). Filers can therefore integration-test in CERT
for two months before go-live.

Supporting **draft implementation guides** are posted on the CATAIR page
(CSMS # 69289734 and # 69298180): the ACE Cargo Release chapter, Cargo
Release condition/error codes, the Entry Summary chapter and Entry Summary
Query, and the entry summary error dictionary (future-capabilities
drafts). These are drafts supporting the CERT deployment — expect revisions
before production, each announced via CSMS; pull the current PDFs from the
[CATAIR page](https://www.cbp.gov/trade/ace/catair) when coding against them.

Two more ET13 drafts followed on **2026-07-29**, both filling in the
PGA side of the waiver — the reason ET13 can carry goods that would otherwise
need formal entry:

- The **PGA Flag Enforcement Table** draft gained an **Entry Type 13
  enforcement flag** (CSMS # 69379627), which is what determines whether a
  flagged HTS line actually blocks release on an ET13 filing.
- The **stand-alone Prior Notice (PE/PX)** CATAIR chapter gained ET13 guidance
  (CSMS # 69379504), so FDA prior notice can be transmitted against an
  informal mail entry.

Both sit in the *Draft Chapters* section — see
[PGA Message Set Filing](/ace-filing/pga-message-set.md).

# IMDW vs ET13

Both share 11 core fields. Differences:

| IMDW (14 fields) | ET13 (12 elements) | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Bond Number | IOR Number | Swapped — ET13 links the bond via the IOR |
| Flight/Conveyance Number | — | Dropped |
| Arrival Date | — | Dropped — ACE derives arrival from the carrier manifest tracking-number match |
