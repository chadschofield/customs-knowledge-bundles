the footer marking it as internally authored. Synthesizes the 2025–2026
regulatory timeline, postal vs non-postal comparisons, client action

supply-chain audits), and employee FAQs.

the primary source (FR 2026-12669) says **10%** — the concepts in this bundle
use 10%.
