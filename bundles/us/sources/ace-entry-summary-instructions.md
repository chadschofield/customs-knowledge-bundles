"U.S. Customs and Border Protection — ACE Entry Summary Instructions",
Version **2.4a**, dated 2016-09-02.

Field-by-field guidance for ACE entry summary data (the 7501 data set):
parties and identifiers, entry type codes, HTSUS line reporting, value and
charges, duty/tax/fee computation, and special-program indicators. Dated but
still the most granular public field reference; where it conflicts with
[ESBP v12.0](ace-esbp-v12.md) or current CATAIR chapters, the newer documents
control.
