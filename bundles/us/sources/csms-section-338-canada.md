CBP filing guidance for the **Section 338 action on Canada** (Tariff Act of
1930, 19 U.S.C. 1338 — duties to offset foreign discrimination against U.S.
commerce): Presidential Proclamations **11046, 11047, and 11048** of
2026-07-20, one each for Canadian discrimination with respect to **alcoholic
beverages, dairy, and motor vehicles**
([91 FR 46639](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/23/2026-14991/imposing-additional-duties-to-offset-canadian-discrimination-against-the-commerce-of-the-united),
[91 FR 46653](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/23/2026-14992/imposing-additional-duties-to-offset-canadian-discrimination-against-the-commerce-of-the-united),
[91 FR 46663](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/23/2026-14997/imposing-additional-duties-to-offset-canadian-discrimination-against-the-commerce-of-the-united)).

Proclamation **11056** (2026-08-18,
[91 FR 54789](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/24/2026-17294/temporary-suspension-of-additional-duties-to-offset-canadian-discrimination-against-the-commerce-of))
**temporarily suspended** the duties from 2026-08-19 until 12:01 a.m. ET on
2026-08-22. This guidance applies to goods entered for consumption, or
withdrawn from warehouse for consumption, on or after **2026-08-22
12:01 a.m. EST**:

| Heading | Scope (U.S. note 51 to subchapter III) | Additional rate |
|---------|----------------------------------------|-----------------|
| 9903.03.12 | Products of Canada per subdivision (b)(1) | **50%** |
| 9903.03.13 | Products of Canada per subdivision (b)(2) | **50%** |
| 9903.03.14 | Products of Canada per subdivision (b)(3) | **50%** |
| 9903.03.15 | Carve-out per subdivision (c): articles already covered by the Section 232 programs (aluminum/steel/copper and derivatives, passenger vehicles and light trucks and their parts, medium/heavy-duty vehicles and parts, wood products, semiconductor articles, patented pharmaceuticals) | 0% |
| 9903.03.16 | Carve-out per subdivision (d): civil aircraft, their engines, parts, components and subassemblies, ground flight simulators | 0% |

Goods under 9903.03.12–.16 remain subject to AD/CVD and all other applicable
duties, taxes, and fees. All five headings were verified live in the published
schedule (2026HTSRev17) on 2026-08-31.
