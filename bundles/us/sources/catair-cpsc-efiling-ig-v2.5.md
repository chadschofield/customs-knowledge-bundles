**CPSC eFiling Implementation Guide**, **Version 2.5**, May 2026. CBP/CPSC
guide for transmitting Consumer Product Safety Commission certificate data
(eFiling) through the PGA Message Set: filing methods, Disclaim A/B rules,
PG19/PG20/PG30/PG60 usage, full vs referenced message set, and registration
via the CPSC Product Registry. At the time of writing CBP lists CPSC eFiling
(v2.5) for **production deployment 2026-07-08**; supporting documents live in
the [CPSC eFiling document library](https://www.cpsc.gov/eFiling-Document-Library).

Supersedes [v2.3](catair-cpsc-efiling-ig-v2.3.md) (v2.4/2.5 clarified OI
record language, added an Intended Use Code restriction for Disclaim B, and
updated Disclaim A scenarios).
