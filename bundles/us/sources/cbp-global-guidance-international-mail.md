Announced 2026-07-08 via **CSMS # 69183472 — "Updated Global Guidance for
International Mail"** (related message 66311990; questions to
CBPDM@cbp.dhs.gov). CBP's operational how-to for the postal informal entry
process that takes effect **2026-07-24** under the mail IFR
(19 CFR Part 145; FR 2026-12669): who may use the process, required data,
bond and payment requirements, and deadlines.

**Local files:**

- [files/cbp-global-guidance-international-mail-2026-07-07.pdf](files/cbp-global-guidance-international-mail-2026-07-07.pdf) —
  the guidance document ("Updated Global Guidance for International
  Mail-7.7.26").
- [files/imdw-international-mail-duty-payment-sheet.xlsx](files/imdw-international-mail-duty-payment-sheet.xlsx) —
  the **official International Mail Duty Worksheet (IMDW) template** filers
  complete and email to CBPDM@cbp.dhs.gov monthly.
