Customs Directive No. **3530-002A**, originating office FO:TP, dated
2001-06-27; supersedes 3530-002 (1984-11-06).

Clarifies Section 484, Tariff Act of 1930 as amended by Pub. L. 97-446: only
the importer of record (owner, purchaser, or designated licensed customs
broker) has the right to make entry; a nominal consignee may designate a
broker but may not self-file, and a broker entering for a nominal consignee
must appear as importer of record. Includes evidence-of-right rules (bills of
lading, air waybills, carrier certificates) and examples.
