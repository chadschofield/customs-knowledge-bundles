**ACE Error Dictionary**, **draft Revision 50**, June 30, 2026 (XLSX).
Identical to [v49](catair-entry-summary-error-dictionary.md) plus one
addition that matters for the 2025–2026 enforcement wave: error
**F875 – IMPORTER INACTIVE FOR ENTRY PURPOSES** — the importer-of-record
number is ineligible to transmit an entry summary. It is the entry-summary
counterpart of cargo release reject 333 (see the
[SE input validation rules](catair-cargo-release-validation-rules.md)).

Deployment history: CBP originally scheduled 2026-07-14; the revision
actually **deployed to production 2026-07-16** (CERT 2026-06-30) per
CSMS # 69268077, and was superseded by
[v51](catair-entry-summary-error-dictionary.md) on 2026-07-17.
