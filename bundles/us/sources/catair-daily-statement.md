**Daily Statement** CATAIR chapter, **Revision 15**, April 23, 2025,
Pub # 0875-0419. Record formats for daily statement processing — the grouping
of entry summaries for consolidated duty payment via ACH. Revision 15
**reduced the number of entry summaries on a statement update from 9,999 to
2,000 "as a result of restriction of de minimis treatment of low value
shipments"** — a direct operational consequence of the
[de minimis suspension](/entry/de-minimis-suspension.md).
