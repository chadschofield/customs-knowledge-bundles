Presidential document, **FR Doc 2025-14897** (90 FR 37775, 2025-08-05). Signed
2025-07-30; effective 2025-08-29 12:01 a.m. EDT.

Suspends the 19 U.S.C. 1321(a)(2)(C) de minimis exemption for all countries
and modes; establishes the transitional postal duty methods (Method A ad
valorem tied to then-IEEPA rates; Method B per-item $80/$160/$200 tiers
through 2026-02-28) and the Qualified Party spreadsheet process; requires
international carrier bonds (19 CFR 113.64) for mail. The postal provisions
were superseded by the 2026 mail IFR; the IEEPA rate basis was invalidated by
*Learning Resources v. Trump* (2026).
