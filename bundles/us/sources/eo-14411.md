Presidential document, **FR Doc 2026-11595** (91 FR 35125, Vol. 91 No. 111,
2026-06-10). Signed 2026-06-03. Implementation deadlines at 45 days
(2026-07-18), 90 days (2026-09-01), 180 days (2026-11-30), one year
(2027-06-03).

Directs rulemaking on IOR eligibility (asset/bonding minimums, foreign-IOR
informal-entry ban, CTPAT requirement), disclosure and certification
(beneficial ownership, foreign-exporter customs filings, CAATSA/18 U.S.C. 545
compliance), penalties (50% floor, no mitigation for repeat offenders, broker
sanctions), and expedited seizure/disposal.
