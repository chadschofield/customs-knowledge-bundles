**Entry Summary Create/Update (AE/AX)** CATAIR chapter, **Revision 109**
(November 6, 2025), Pub # 0875-0419, 246 pages. Changes over
[rev 108](catair-entry-summary-ae-ax-rev-108.md): Global Business Identifier
(GBI) groupings repeat four times (previously three), the SE31/SE51
"Identifier" field grows from 20 to 35 characters, and a new ALTA/Altana GBI
qualifier is allowed.

At the time of writing CBP lists this revision as published (11/06/2025) with
**production deployment TBD** — file to rev 108 until CBP announces the
cutover.
