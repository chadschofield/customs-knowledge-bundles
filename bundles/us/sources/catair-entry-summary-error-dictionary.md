**ACE Error Dictionary** (CBP lists it as "Error Dictionary: Entry Summary"),
**Revision 49**, June 25, 2026 (XLSX). Roughly a thousand condition codes
returned on entry summary (AE/AX) filings, each with narrative text, an
explanation, and its last-updated date. Rev 49's addition is enforcement- and
tariff-flavored: error **876 – DUTY HTS REQUIRES NON-DUTY HTS**, rejecting
auto-parts entry summary lines that declare the Section 232 duty HTS without
the paired non-duty HTS.

A [draft revision 50](catair-entry-summary-error-dictionary-v50-draft.md) is
scheduled to deploy 2026-07-14.
