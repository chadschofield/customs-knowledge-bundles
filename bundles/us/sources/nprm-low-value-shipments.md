Notice of proposed rulemaking. **FR Doc 2026-20650**, **91 FR 64532**,
published **2026-10-08**. Docket **USCBP-2026-0298**, RIN 1685-AA38.
**Comments are due 2026-12-07** via regulations.gov. It proposes the
permanent low-value regime that follows the 2026 interim rules. The main
proposals (amending 19 CFR parts 113, 128, 141, 143, 145):

- **Entry Type 11 goes electronic and bonded.** Filers must transmit the
  3461/7501 (or 7501) data by EDI (§143.23). Release requires an STB or
  continuous §113.62 bond (new §143.29). Entry must be filed **on or before
  the date of importation** (§141.5).
- **Chapter 99 goods can enter informally up to $2,500.** The $250 cap for
  Chapter 99 subchapter III/IV goods (Section 232/301 and similar) is removed
  (§§141.82(d), 143.21, 143.23(d)).
- **Blanket formal-entry requirements.** CBP could require formal entry by
  importer of record, manufacturer or seller, or merchandise category, not
  just case by case (§143.22).
- **Broker as importer of record.** Consignees that are not owners or
  purchasers must appoint a broker to act as IOR (§143.26).
- **Mail: ET13 replaces the IMDW worksheet.** Mail of $2,500 or less must be
  entered electronically, on or before importation (§145.12(b)), with a
  bond (§145.15). Mail not entered within **15 days is deemed abandoned** and
  goes back to USPS (new §145.6).
- **Carrier manifest data.** Carriers must report the **UPU S10 tracking
  number** matching the entry on the inward manifest for every mode,
  including express consignments (new §145.7).
- **Bond damages.** Default on an informal-entry bond costs the merchandise
  value or **$1,000, whichever is greater**, or three times that for
  restricted or prohibited goods or alcohol (§113.62(n)(6)).
- **Bona fide gifts** keep their existing entry procedure (§128.24(f)).
