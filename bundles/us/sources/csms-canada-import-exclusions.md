CBP guidance (CSMS # 70050970, 2026-09-28) implementing Presidential
Proclamations **11061, 11062, and 11063** — "Excluding Certain Canadian
[Alcoholic Beverages / Products] from Importation into the United States in
Response to Continued Discrimination Against the Commerce of the United
States" with respect to **alcoholic beverages, motor vehicles, and dairy**.
These escalate the [Section 338 duties](csms-section-338-canada.md) on the
same three sectors from a 50% duty to an outright **exclusion from
importation**.

Key terms as stated in the guidance:

- **Effective 2026-09-29 12:01 a.m. ET.** Covered products are excluded from
  entry for consumption, **FTZ admission, bonded-warehouse entry, and in-bond
  transportation**. Entries containing covered products are **unconditionally
  rejected**; entries filed in ACE for covered products not yet released are
  **cancelled** at the effective time.
- Goods **imported before** the effective time remain eligible for entry;
  goods already in a bonded warehouse or FTZ may be withdrawn for consumption
  at the 50% Section 338 duty (Proclamations 11046–11048).
- Covered headings: **0404, 1702, 1703, 2202, 2203–2208, and 8711**.
- **Alcohol scope limitation:** lines marked "Packaged" cover only products in
  bottles, cans, boxes, kegs, or similar **direct-to-consumption containers**;
  alcohol outside a scope limitation stays subject to the 50% duty under
  Proclamation 11046 instead.
- **New reject codes** from the same moment: **239** (e214 FTZ admission) and
  **335** (cargo release), both "HTS NOT ALLOWED FOR COUNTRY OF ORIGIN"
  (also CSMS # 70050117), and **886 – HTS PROHIBITED FOR CO** on entry
  summary.
