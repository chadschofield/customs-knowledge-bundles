General notice modifying the [Entry Type 13 test](notice-entry-type-13.md)
(91 FR 38007). **FR Doc 2026-21570**, **91 FR 66707**, published and
effective **2026-10-22**. It confirms the test began on **2026-09-22**.

The modification: ET13 filers may transmit an entry **with or without
complete electronic PGA data**. This is temporary, so filers can start
using ET13 while they build out full PGA submissions. CBP will announce the
end of this allowance in a future Federal Register notice. Everything else
stays the same. ET13 remains an alternative to the IMDW process (19 CFR
145.12(b)) for mail of $2,500 or less, including goods with PGA
requirements or non-Chapter 1–97 duties (for example Section 232, 201 or
301). Goods under Chapter 98/99 or with FTA claims must still transmit the
extra data those provisions require.
