Final rule; extension of compliance date. **FR Doc 2026-21600**,
**91 FR 66638**, published **2026-10-22**, the day the original deadline
fell. It was issued without notice and comment under the APA good-cause
exception.

It amends nothing substantive in the
[mail IFR](ifr-mail-de-minimis.md) (91 FR 37801). It moves only the
compliance date for **19 CFR 145.12(a)(2)(v) and (vi)** from **2026-10-22
to 2027-01-20**. Those provisions make mail ineligible for the postal
informal entry (IMDW) process if it is subject to **PGA requirements** or
**Chapter 98/99 duties**, or claims Chapter 98 or **FTA** duty-free
treatment. Until the new date:

- The IMDW process stays available for those goods, unchanged.
- Parties may comply early, by formal entry or the
  [Entry Type 13 test](notice-entry-type-13.md).
- CBP's stated reason is that importers, brokers and postal logistics
  providers need more time to build systems, procedures and data
  infrastructure, and that enforcing the date risked disrupting mail flows.

CBP announced this in the Federal Register only; no CSMS message carried it.
