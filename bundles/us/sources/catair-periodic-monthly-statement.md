**Periodic Monthly Statement** CATAIR chapter, March 6, 2019, Pub 0875-0419.
Output record descriptions (Q1/Q2/QA/Q3/Q4/QE/Q5 records) and processing rules
for **PMS** — the program that consolidates eligible entry summaries into a
single monthly statement, deferring duty payment to the 15th working day of
the following month.
