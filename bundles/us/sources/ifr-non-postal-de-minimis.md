Interim final rule with request for comments. **FR Doc 2026-12670**; Docket
USCBP-2026-0760; CBP Dec. 26-12; RIN 1685-AA44. Published and effective
2026-06-24 (Vol. 91 No. 121). Amends 19 CFR 10.151 (adds paragraph (b)).

Key content: indefinite non-postal de minimis suspension; suspension of the
Entry Type 86 test; elimination of release-from-manifest
(19 CFR 143.23(j)(3)) for low-value imports; transition to Entry Type 11 or
formal entry with full duties, 10-digit HTSUS, and bonds; explicitly adds no
new commercial data elements; preserves gift ($100) and traveler ($200)
exemptions.
