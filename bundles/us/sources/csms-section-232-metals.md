CBP filing guidance for **Presidential Proclamation 11021** (2026-04-02),
"Strengthening Actions Taken to Adjust Imports of Aluminum, Steel, and Copper
into the United States," under Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962
(19 U.S.C. 1862). Imposes **10–50% additional duties** on the full customs
value of covered metal articles and derivatives from all countries, effective
**2026-04-06** (91 FR 18201). A good containing more than one covered metal is
subject to only one of the rates; derivatives outside HTS Chapters 72/73/74/76
with under 15% covered-metal content are excluded on the non-metal value.

Announced via **CSMS # 68253075** (2026-04-03); Annex IV **technical
corrections** issued 2026-04-29 and explained in **CSMS # 68554727**
(2026-05-06). Duties are declared through Chapter 99 secondary HTS lines; the
covered tariff numbers are enumerated in the attached lists.
