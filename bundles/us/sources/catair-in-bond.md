**In-Bond** CATAIR implementation guide, **Amendment 51**, April 2026,
Pub # 0875-0419, 72 pages. Record formats for ACE in-bond processing: in-bond
bill of lading input/output records against ACE eManifest (Air, Sea, Rail)
and ACE Truck bills, in-bond update/transfer-of-liability records,
arrival/export transactions, and the status notifications that report them
(**QP** filer transactions). Errors are catalogued separately in
[Appendix T](catair-appendix-t-in-bond-errors.md).

Note the landing page's URL slug is `bond` — it is the in-bond chapter, not
the [eBond chapter](catair-ebond-cb-cx.md).
