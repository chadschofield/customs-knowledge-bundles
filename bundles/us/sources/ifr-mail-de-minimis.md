Interim final rule with request for comments. **FR Doc 2026-12669**; Docket
USCBP-2026-0761; CBP Dec. 26-13; RIN 1685-AA45. Published 2026-06-24
(Vol. 91 No. 121). Effective: suspension (19 CFR 145.31) immediately;
postal informal entry process + bond (new 19 CFR 145.15) 2026-07-24;
PGA/Ch. 98-99 formal-entry compliance 2026-10-22. Amends 19 CFR Part 145
(§§ 145.12, 145.15, 145.31).

Key content: indefinite mail de minimis suspension; the 14-field
International Mail Duty Worksheet emailed to CBPDM@cbp.dhs.gov by the 7th of
the following month with Pay.gov payment; licensed-broker filer requirement;
exclusions (AD/CVD, quota, alcohol/tobacco, PGA, Ch. 98/99); Section 122
context (10% surcharge); OBBBA statutory repeal 2027-07-01; impact analysis
projecting postal volume decline.
