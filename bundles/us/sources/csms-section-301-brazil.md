CBP filing guidance for the **USTR Section 301 action on Brazil** (Trade Act
of 1974; 91 FR 45516, FR Doc
[2026-14542](https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2026-14542) — the
digital-trade and electronic-payments investigation): **25% additional ad
valorem duty on all products of Brazil**, effective for goods entered or
withdrawn for consumption on or after **2026-07-22 12:01 a.m. ET**, declared
via Chapter 99 heading **9903.05.01**.

Exemptions ride headings **9903.05.02–9903.05.09**, including:

- **In-transit window (9903.05.02):** goods loaded and in transit on the
  final mode before 2026-07-22 *and* entered before **2026-07-29**.
- **Product exemptions (9903.05.03–.04 …):** the HTSUS classifications in
  U.S. note 50 to subchapter III — enumerated in the attached list — plus
  named carve-outs (e.g. certain religious articles, specified tropical
  fruit lines).

The corresponding ACE tariff records loaded via **HSU 2616** (created
2026-07-21, CSMS # 69324417).
