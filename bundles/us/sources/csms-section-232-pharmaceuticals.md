CBP filing guidance for **Presidential Proclamation 11020** (2026-04-02,
"Adjusting Imports of Pharmaceuticals and Pharmaceutical Ingredients Into the
United States"; [91 FR 18183](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/09/2026-06956/adjusting-imports-of-pharmaceuticals-and-pharmaceutical-ingredients-into-the-united-states)),
imposing Section 232 duties on **patented pharmaceuticals** and their
ingredients (active pharmaceutical ingredients and key starting materials).
Declared via Chapter 99 headings **9903.04.60–9903.04.69**; covered primary
classifications sit in **HTSUS Chapters 29 and 30**.

**Generic pharmaceuticals and their ingredients are not subject** to the
additional duties (9903.04.67).

# Two-Stage Effective Date

| Products of | Duties begin |
|---|---|
| Companies listed in **Annex III** to the Proclamation | **2026-07-31** 12:01 a.m. ET |
| **All other companies** | **2026-09-29** 12:01 a.m. ET |

**Reporting is required from 2026-07-31 regardless of company.** Every
importer of goods under the covered Chapter 29/30 classifications must report
an applicable Chapter 99 heading from that date; non-Annex-III importers file
the **zero-duty heading 9903.04.61** for entries from 2026-07-31 through
2026-09-28.

# Headings and Rates

| Heading | Applies to | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **9903.04.60** | Patented pharmaceutical articles generally (U.S. note 40(c),(d)), except as provided in 9903.04.61 | **100%** — combined column one + Section 232 |
| **9903.04.61** | Patented articles of companies *not* in Annex III, entered before 2026-09-29 (note 40(c),(e)) | 0% additional |
| **9903.04.62** | Products of Japan, EU member states, South Korea, Switzerland, Liechtenstein | **15%** combined |
| **9903.04.63** | Products of the United Kingdom (note 40(c),(g)) | **0% additional** — reduced from 10% effective 2026-07-31 (see below) |
| **9903.04.64** | Articles covered by a qualifying onshoring plan | 20%, escalating to 100% on 2030-04-02 — **no eligible companies at the time of writing** |
| **9903.04.65** | Onshoring plan + MFN pricing agreement | 0% additional; expires 2029-01-20 |
| **9903.04.66** | Pharmaceuticals for specified uses | 0% additional |
| **9903.04.67** | **Generic** pharmaceutical articles | 0% additional |
| **9903.04.68** | US-origin active pharmaceutical ingredients returned in dosage form | 0% additional |
| **9903.04.69** | Non-pharmaceutical articles otherwise classified in Chapters 29–30 | 0% additional |

Stacking rules: the duties apply **in addition to** any special (FTA) rate,
but where a product is subject to more than one rate under the Proclamation,
**the lowest applicable rate applies**.

# United Kingdom Reduction (CSMS # 69415934)

Commerce's **2026-07-31** notice, "Reduction of Tariffs on Patented
Pharmaceuticals and Pharmaceutical Ingredients for Products of the United
Kingdom Implemented by Presidential Proclamation 11020"
([FR Doc 2026-15799](https://www.federalregister.gov/public-inspection/2026-15799/reduction-of-tariffs-patented-pharmaceuticals-and-pharmaceutical-ingredients-for-products-of-the)),
cut heading **9903.04.63** from **10% to 0%** additional ad valorem,
**effective 2026-07-31** — the same date the duties began, so UK patented
pharmaceuticals never paid the 10%. CBP completed the ACE updates.

> **The published HTS lags the reduction.** Verified 2026-08-03 against the
> [current HTS export](hts.md) (2026HTSRev14): 9903.04.63 still reads
> "The duty provided in the applicable subheading **+ 10%**". The reduction
> lives in the Commerce notice and CBP's ACE configuration, not yet in the
> tariff schedule. Quote the CSMS and the FR notice for UK patented
> pharmaceuticals, not the HTS line, until a later revision catches up.

The covered Chapter 29 and 30 classifications corresponding to each Chapter 99
heading are in the attached list, reflecting the
[July 1, 2026 484(f) Committee changes](https://www.usitc.gov/tariff_affairs/documents/list_of_committee_changes_for_july_1_2026-final.pdf)
to the HTSUS.

The corresponding ACE tariff records loaded via **HSU 2618** (created
2026-07-31, CSMS # 69403181 — 174 tariff and 591 ABI records).
