Customs value is the base for ad valorem duties, and with the
[de minimis suspension](/entry/de-minimis-suspension.md) every parcel's
declared value now drives a real duty calculation.
[EO 14411](/enforcement/eo-14411-enforcement.md) explicitly targets
undervaluation, so valuation discipline is an enforcement issue, not just an
accounting one. US law (19 U.S.C. 1401a; 19 CFR Part 152) implements the WTO
Customs Valuation Agreement.

# The Method Hierarchy (apply in order)

1. **Transaction value** — the price actually paid or payable for the goods
   when sold for exportation to the US, plus statutory additions. The method
   for the overwhelming majority of entries.
2. **Transaction value of identical merchandise**
3. **Transaction value of similar merchandise**
4. **Deductive value** — US resale price minus post-import costs.
5. **Computed value** — production cost + profit/general expenses. (Importer
   may elect to try computed before deductive.)
6. **Fallback** — a prior method reasonably adjusted (19 U.S.C. 1401a(f)).

Transaction value is unavailable when the sale is between **related parties**
and the relationship influenced the price (circumstances-of-sale / test-values
analysis), or when restrictions/conditions make the price unascertainable.

# Statutory Additions to the Price (when not already included)

- Packing costs incurred by the buyer
- Selling commissions incurred by the buyer
- **Assists** — materials, components, tooling, dies, molds, or engineering/
  design work (done outside the US) the buyer supplies free or at reduced cost
- Royalties and license fees the buyer must pay as a condition of sale
- Proceeds of subsequent resale that accrue to the seller

Ecommerce pitfalls: "first sale" claims in multi-tier transactions require a
bona fide sale for export at that tier and documentary proof; discounted
platform prices, subsidized shipping, and related-party fulfillment
structures all invite value questions.

# The Valuation Encyclopedia

The **[CBP Valuation Encyclopedia (1980–2021)](/sources/valuation-encyclopedia.md)**
(local copy in this bundle) is CBP's compendium of valuation rulings and
policy digests, organized by topic (assists, computed value, related parties,
royalties, transaction value…). Use it to find precedent on a fact pattern,
then pull the full ruling from
[CROSS](/regulations/cross-rulings.md) for the authoritative, current text —
and check it hasn't been revoked.
